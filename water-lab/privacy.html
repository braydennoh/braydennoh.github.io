Water Lab Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-14

Water Lab is a fluid physics sandbox app. The app itself does not require an account, does not ask for any personal information, and does not send your simulation data anywhere. Your settings and purchase state are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The free version of Water Lab shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), approximate location inferred from IP address, and usage data to serve and measure ads. On iOS, personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are non-personalized. You can review Google's practices at policies.google.com.

Consent

Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), a consent form from Google is shown before any personalized ads load, and you can change your choice at any time from within the ad experience.

Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details. Purchasing Pro removes all ads, which also stops all AdMob data collection described above.

Children

Water Lab is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
