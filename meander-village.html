Meander Village

Settlements on meandering river floodplains face a fundamental tension: the river creates fertile land, but also erodes it away. Soil quality decays exponentially: \(q(\mathbf{x}, t) = 0.5^{(t - t_{\text{last}}) / T_{1/2}}\). A household must relocate when \(q < 0.5\) or when the river erodes its cell. It searches outward in expanding Chebyshev rings until finding an unoccupied cell with \(q \geq 0.5\). The Chebyshev distance \(d = \max(|\Delta r|, |\Delta c|)\) measures displacement effort.

At low density, most moves are \(d = 1\) (local). As population approaches the carrying capacity (~2200 habitable cells), displacement distances explode. Press Play and adjust the population slider. By Brayden Noh.
