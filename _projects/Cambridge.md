My primary source of being active is running outside since I started my PhD at Harvard. Cambridge is such an ideal city to run because the running path follows the Charles River tightly (like exactly next to it), and the weather is cold and fresh enough for my runs. A typical run would start at Harvard, follow the river toward the city, and you would reach MIT; I typically cross the Charles River at the Harvard Bridge, keep going to Beacon Hill, and sometimes see Quincy Market. It is, however, getting quite cold, and the limited sun has made it harder to run on weekdays since November started. Nevertheless, I feel very lucky to live in such an active city.
