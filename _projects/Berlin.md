In 2025, I started my PhD at Harvard. Four years ago, when I was starting at Caltech, I made this same trip to Berlin. My freshman reasoning was simple. I speak German, but I quickly realized that was a bit pointless as everyone here speaks Englis. But Berlin is about much more than language. Coming from Los Angeles, where a twenty-year-old restaurant is considered "old," this city gives a new meaning to the word. The city is deeply inflicted with history, both dark and bright. It is fascinating to see how the remnants of different regimes still control the look of the city. From the pre-war era to the World War, the Soviet era, and the modern day, the city has everything. I went for a run to see it for myself. I started in West Berlin, near my hotel, ran through the Brandenburg Gate, and finished in East Berlin where the TV Tower stands. Even now, the change in scenery is very noticeable. You can see exactly where one world ended and the other began.
